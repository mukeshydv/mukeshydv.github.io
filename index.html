About Me

Staff Engineer & Mobile Systems Architect

I am Mukesh Yadav, a Staff Engineer currently leading mobile architecture and SDK development at Mindtickle in Pune, India. With 10+ years of dedicated experience in the mobile industry, I specialize in native iOS engineering (Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit, RxSwift, Combine), Kotlin Multiplatform (KMM), Golang microservices, and distributed observability.
